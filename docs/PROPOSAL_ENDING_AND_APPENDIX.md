# Proposal ending and appendix policy

**Adopted: 2026-11-16. This supersedes the previous three-slide experiment section and standalone references/Q&A page.**

- In a thesis **proposal**, include exactly **one audience-facing experimental plan slide**. Summarize planned data types/eligibility, train/search/test split and comparison, metrics, and explicitly mark all outcomes as unmeasured.
- **No standalone References slide** during the proposal. Do not remove readable author/year attribution from relevant literature content; retain bibliographic traceability in the thesis bibliography and private speaking notes.
- End the main presentation with a dedicated **Thank You / Q&A slide**. Any **Appendix must come AFTER** that slide and is shown only in response to questions.
- Appendices are technical backups, not planned oral narrative. Prioritize: **(A) fit/search/test roles and leakage prevention; (B) valid randomized MNB parameters and PSO; (C) JMH and feature-weight iterations.**
- The revised ending consists of Slides **26 Experiment Plan, 27 Expected Contributions, 28 Thank You, 29 Appendix A, 30 Appendix B, 31 Appendix C**. A 31-slide master is acceptable because only 28 slides precede the appendix.
- Preserve the existing Canva canonical project; create safety backup of edited pages before any structural replacement. Avoid modifying Slides 1–25 solely to regenerate a footer.
- **Footer navigation should use 附錄 instead of 參考文獻 throughout.** Earlier slides retain old wording until a previewed Canva text-edit transaction is specifically approved and committed. Mark this as pending, not done.
- Technical formulas in appendix slides remain editable placeholders in Canva; final latex→Office Math (OMML) conversion occurs after Canva export.
- Do not commit unpublished slide content, file binaries, detailed formulas, speaking notes, or Canva edit-access URLs to the public GitHub repository.
